## Story structure

- Setup: a roadside stop and initial safety questions.
- Context: adults explain their travel and living circumstances.
- Reveal: officers inspect the crowded vehicle/trailer.
- Escalation: discussion turns to heat, water, occupancy, and child welfare.
- Response: medical personnel arrive and children are assessed.

## Editorial safeguards

- Protect minors' faces, names, and identifying details.
- Preserve source context and distinguish allegations from established facts.
- Do not add sensational captions or misleading hooks.
- Confirm permission or rights to republish the source footage.
- Keep publishing under human control.
